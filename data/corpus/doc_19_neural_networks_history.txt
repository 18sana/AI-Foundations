The history of neural networks dates back to the 1940s with the work of Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts. In 1958, Frank Rosenblatt invented the Perceptron, which could learn to classify simple patterns. However, interest waned after Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert published a book in 1969 proving that single-layer perceptrons could not solve non-linearly separable problems like XOR. The field was revitalized in the 1980s with the popularization of the backpropagation training algorithm.
