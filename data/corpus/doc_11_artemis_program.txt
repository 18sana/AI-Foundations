NASA's Artemis program aims to land the first woman and the next man on the Moon. The program utilizes the Space Launch System (SLS) rocket and the Orion spacecraft. Artemis I, an uncrewed test flight around the Moon, was successfully completed in late 2022. Artemis II will carry a crewed flight path around the Moon, and Artemis III is planned to land astronauts on the lunar south pole, targeting sustainable lunar exploration.
