In December 2022, scientists at the Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory's National Ignition Facility (NIF) achieved fusion ignition for the first time. Fusion ignition occurs when the energy produced by fusion reactions exceeds the laser energy delivered to the target. The experiment delivered 2.05 megajoules of energy and yielded 3.15 megajoules of fusion energy, demonstrating a net energy gain (Q > 1).
