While NASA officially announced that Voyager 1 crossed the heliopause in August 2012, some astrophysicists disputed the exact timing. They argued that the magnetic field data did not show the expected shift in direction. A subsequent analysis of plasma density waves published in 2013 suggested that the entry into interstellar space actually occurred in May 2013 when a solar storm caused the surrounding plasma to vibrate, providing a clear measurement of the local density.
