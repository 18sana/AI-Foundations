The Transformer is a deep learning model introduced in the 2017 paper 'Attention Is All You Need'. Unlike older recurrent neural networks (RNNs), the Transformer relies entirely on self-attention mechanisms to model global dependencies between input and output tokens. This allows for significantly more parallelization during training. The core components include multi-head self-attention, positional encodings, and feed-forward neural layers, forming the foundation of modern Large Language Models.
